Terms of Service

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Scope

Quran Daily Poster is a single-operator tool. It uploads Quranic recitation videos to the TikTok account of the person running it. There are no other users and no public service.

Acceptable use

The operator agrees to publish only Quranic recitation content and to comply with TikTok's Community Guidelines and Terms of Service. Uploads happen only to the account that granted authorization.

Content sources

Quranic text and recitation audio are retrieved from the public quran.com API and attributed in the video. Fonts used are licensed under the SIL Open Font License.

No warranty

The tool is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. The operator is solely responsible for what is published.

Termination

Authorization may be revoked at any time from TikTok account settings, which stops all uploads.
